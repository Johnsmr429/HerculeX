## From CT to readable papyrus

1. **Acquire and bind the volume.** Synchrotron propagation phase-contrast micro-CT captures sheets, fibers and, in favorable protocols, deposits associated with ink. Energy, propagation distance and voxel size all matter. A high-resolution subscan can have a different frame from a lower-resolution full scan of the same object. Record volume ID, pyramid level, physical spacing, axis order, and registration before combining any annotations, mesh, or labels. Registration quality matters at the scale of the sampling window. [Scanning at scale](https://scrollprize.substack.com/p/scanning-herculaneum-scrolls-at-scale) · [Open data format and registrations](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/open-data) · [Scroll 4 methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29085v1)
2. **Extract local evidence.** Predict a recto or medial surface, directional normals, layer density, fibers, affinities, or a local winding phase. A thin sheet mask can be excellent on average while joining two turns at a single bit of compressed papyrus. Trace the evidence into curves or quad patches, inspect those in CT, and preserve validity and confidence. Recto and verso are two plies of one sheet, typically with crossed fibers. In the Challenge's operational convention the recto faces the umbilicus where identifiable. A local normal has two possible signs until oriented. [Anatomy](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/SCROLL_ANATOMY.md) · [Open problems](https://scrollprize.org/2026_open_problems)
3. **Recover sheet identity.** A turn of the roll shares a physical sheet with its neighbors around the seam, yet they are distinct local layers in a cross-section. Pick a consistent seam, centerline and handedness. Keep same-turn paths, relative differences, absolute anchors, and ambiguous evidence explicit. A relative edge measures a difference under that convention. Along a horizontal fiber the unwrapped angle can advance through the cut even though the physical fiber is continuous. Represent wrap transport at seam crossings before adding edges or counting cycles. A consistent component with only relative edges still has an arbitrary offset. [Winding constraints](https://scrollprize.org/open_problems/winding_annotations) · [Fiber graph coordinates](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/vesuvius/docs/fiber_layout_coordinates.md)
4. **Choose a reconstruction strategy.** Patch-first workflows follow the CT closely and expose gaps as gaps. They need robust stitching and verification of winding and seam topology. A fitted spiral ties sparse observations into a full scroll through a common, invertible deformation. Its interpolated geometry needs review in crushed, torn or underconstrained areas. Fiber-first workflows can use long fibers to span regions where local growth struggles, then assign winding and UV from the measured relationships. A hybrid can splice verified geometry into an agreeing global fit or refine a local region after scouting. [Spiral tutorial](https://scrollprize.org/tutorial_spiral) · [Fiber winding graph](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/vesuvius/docs/wgraph.md) · [ScrollFiesta](https://github.com/Hob3rMallow/scrollfiesta_public)
5. **Flatten and sample.** Keep a map `(u,v) → (x,y,z)` and a validity mask. A tifxyz stores the XYZ coordinates in three aligned TIFFs on a regular 2D grid with metadata. The grid may still be stretched, flipped, or on the wrong layer. Flatten with an explicit metric objective or fiber coordinates, rasterize the UV result back to scan coordinates, and sample a CT stack along consistently oriented normals. In physical units the sample is `CT(p(u,v) + d·n(u,v))`. Record which side and depth zero were used, voxel spacing, depth order, and interpolation. Sample through the uncertainty band when scouting. [Data formats](https://scrollprize.org/data) · [Scroll 4 flattening and rendering](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29085v1#Sx4)
6. **Recover ink and read it.** Surface-conditioned readers consume the depth stack and output a 2D probability image. A native volumetric model outputs XYZ ink probabilities, which can be projected onto an independently tracked surface. Pooled fine scans and native coarse scans at the same nominal voxel size can behave differently. Keep the raw CT view and depth stack beside the model overlay. Validate candidate strokes for persistence across nearby depths and independent renderings, plausible column geometry, and physical correspondence to the same location. The scholarly reading follows expert inspection of the images and geometry. [Ink tutorial](https://scrollprize.org/tutorial5) · [First Letters workflow](https://scrollprize.substack.com/p/from-ct-scan-to-ancient-text-a-first) · [Scroll 4 study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29085)

### What to measure at each handoff

| Handoff | Check |
| --- | --- |
| CT → evidence | Hold out whole volumes or scrolls and record whether they informed preprocessing, hyperparameters or checkpoint selection. Measure missed sheets and false bridges in compressed areas and across orientations. |
| Evidence → winding IDs | Report retained coverage and the distribution of relative errors. Hold out independent fibers, winding ladders, or expert surfaces. Report unassigned cases, stripe-wise offsets, and inconsistent loops. |
| Winding IDs → global fit | Check same-turn consistency, wrong-wrap fraction, seam order, and distance to independent geometry. Separate CT-supported regions from interpolation and review missing or torn tissue explicitly. |
| Mesh → UV and stack | Check metric distortion, foldovers, self-intersections, holes, normal flips, and XYZ↔UV correspondence. At a seam, verify that both geometry and writing stay continuous. |
| Stack → ink → reading | Compare matched XYZ regions at comparable physical depths, and use blank papyrus and held-out scrolls. Track false ink at matched recall alongside Dice and AUC. Preserve model, checkpoint, geometry and render provenance for every candidate. |

An exact graph optimum certifies the supplied measurements and objective. In particular, a coherent-looking result can be one winding off or compress several windings into one. Score registration, topology, UV distortion and ink separately. The `winding-phase` project shows a case where changing the geometry-assignment rule changes the comparison between two fits. [Winding-sync limitations](https://github.com/abundantjoe/winding-sync#limitations) · [Winding-phase evaluation](https://github.com/millerandmuller/winding-phase#the-result-depends-on-the-scorer)

## Windings and solvers

A constraint graph has distinct jobs even when its inputs share the same geometry. Normal-sign synchronization orients measurements. Winding synchronization estimates relative integer turns. UV solves assign positions and scale along and across the sheet after the turns are established. A graph may contain contradictory cycles, disconnected components, and noninteger measurements that require rounding or a different objective. Fitting a smooth field in scan coordinates is another route to the winding coordinate, with explicit tests needed for layer spacing and the seam. [Villa fiber workflows](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/vesuvius/docs/fiber_workflows.md)

## Global fit

The spiral fitter estimates a canonical wound sheet and a smooth invertible map into the scan. The model uses an integrated stationary velocity field, global scale and shear, and locally adjustable winding gaps. The linked tutorial describes Adam. The pinned villa code constructs a fused AdamW variant and groups parameters for its fit schedule. Evidence includes distance terms, winding relationships, normals, density and boundary anchors. The parameterization supplies ordering, while CT evidence determines whether the surface actually follows the intended papyrus. [Spiral-fitting tutorial](https://scrollprize.org/tutorial_spiral) · [Pinned optimizer](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/0bddf2757ea1e97572e86d06d448d8e4a7e207b3/spiral-fitting/fit_spiral.py#L2456) · [WACV paper and supplement](https://github.com/pmh47/spiral-fitting)

### First fit and spacing refinement

A new scan needs an umbilicus and a consistent coordinate frame for its selected evidence. In the pinned configuration `input_use_tracks` is off, so supplying a track file alone leaves track losses inactive. The default `dense_spacing_mode` is `winding_model`. Its compact crossing store is generated from rays seeded on a preliminary fit. For a fresh fit without this store, use the tutorial's `grad_mag` route with the appropriate gradient input or explicitly disable winding inference. Treat predicted fiber directions as orientation evidence. An attached and checked fiber trace can provide long-range identity, while a local direction sample cannot tell which nearby winding it belongs to. ## Full unwrapping

## Ink detection

The conventional input is an oriented stack around a surface. For each model preserve the actual native or pooled voxel size, normal sign, layer convention, input depth and normalization. A 17-plane recipe at roughly 9 µm spans a much wider physical band than 17 planes at 2.4 µm. Centering, resampling, overlap and inference-time augmentation can change a comparison. For generalized ink scouting, prefer genuinely held-out scrolls and test blank regions and adjacent-sheet controls. ## Evidence checks and corrections

The audit checks method descriptions against source code, official reports and model cards, and checks release status against upstream. Each measured result below is scoped to its named data and protocol. The source audit leaves full CT reconstruction runs and model-training experiments unreplicated.

### Geometry and winding

- **Cycle consistency needs seam transport.** Take A→B as +1 and B→C as +1. A direct A→C label of +3 leaves a one-turn discrepancy once all three edges use the same seam convention. This flags a loop for review. With no further evidence, any implicated annotation can be the source. Villa’s checkpoint-based diagnostic calculates within-patch transport along valid quads and accounts for the point-to-patch attachment crossing. The pre-fit windaudit experiment found a false contradiction when that attachment term was omitted in an earlier revision. Its certificates apply to the measured graph and its coordinate assumptions. [Pinned Villa diagnostic](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/0bddf2757ea1e97572e86d06d448d8e4a7e207b3/spiral-fitting/find_inconsistent_windings.py) · [windaudit report](https://github.com/sergeievland/windaudit)
- **Exact winding objectives differ.** HerculeX’s network-simplex solution concerns its weighted integer patch and ray measurements, including an asymmetric cost for ray residuals. Villa’s standard `vc_wgraph winding` solves a fractional-target L1 problem with GPU IRLS and reads out integer labels. Villa’s network-simplex/LEMON comparison has its own experimental entry point. Any agreement between these solvers depends on the observations, weighting, allowed assignment range and seam convention. [HerculeX integer levels](../README.md#exact-integer-levels) · [Villa default solver](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/vesuvius/docs/wgraph.md) · [Villa flow experiments](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/main/vesuvius/docs/fiber_min_flow_experiments.md)
- **A fit score also measures the assignment rule.** In `winding-phase`, a nearest-vertex fallback can mark a point unassigned when it lies on the interior of a valid cell with widely spaced vertices. A looser nearest-surface search can instead claim a point on the adjacent winding when the correct mesh has a hole. In the spiralfiteval author’s planted-hole test on re-made Paris 4 fits, 18 of 38 point assignments went to the neighboring winding, all beside holes at least 2×2 vertices. Report assigned-pair error, correct pairs over all labeled pairs, and valid-cell coverage together. For disputed points, check both candidate surfaces and the source CT in the same frame. [Scorer comparison](https://github.com/millerandmuller/winding-phase#the-result-depends-on-the-scorer) · [Planted-hole test](https://github.com/jo-tud/spiralfiteval)
- **A near attachment can hide a switch downstream.** In the Paris 4 z 15,000–17,500 test band of the fiber–patch continuity report, the author flagged 179 of 347 linked pairs for wider-path divergence. Dropping suspect links or patches failed to improve the fit on held-out fibers in their experiment. This probe provides cases for review, with the nine held-out fibers and fit recipe limiting the inference. [Fiber–patch continuity experiment](https://github.com/TAUIL-Abd-Elilah/fiber-patch-continuity)
- **A consistent normal field can have the wrong polarity.** Reversing a UV axis reverses the sign of `∂P/∂U × ∂P/∂V`. The renderer’s `--flip-normals` changes depth ordering, so a model’s “forward” pass depends on the mesh and rendering convention. In the PHerc. 0125 review, a radial orientation heuristic was unreliable where the roll was crushed almost flat. An eligible-mesh ink release independently documented swapped forward/reverse filenames after rendering without normal reversal. Use matched local CT and fiber evidence to establish the side. Keep both passes labeled by the actual sampled direction. [Renderer implementation](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/blob/0bddf2757ea1e97572e86d06d448d8e4a7e207b3/volume-cartographer/apps/src/vc_render_tifxyz.cpp#L842) · [PHerc. 0125 review](https://discord.com/channels/1079907749569237093/1568295516943683605) · [Released maps and direction correction](https://huggingface.co/datasets/pscamillo/vesuvius-eligible-meshes-ink9)

### Reproduced geometry control

I ran the three synthetic demo tests in the pinned spiralcheck source. The clean eight-winding fixture had a minimum sheet-consistency score of 1.0 with zero intrinsic violations and zero collapsed samples. The same fixture with a planted winding swap and gap collapse scored 0.636 minimum sheet consistency and flagged 80 intrinsic violations and 60 collapsed samples. Re-running the defective fixture produced the same metrics. This checks the evaluator’s response to known defects on idealized data. [Pinned demo tests](https://github.com/Nicodol/spiralcheck/blob/d1b50e2957409a870225fb9f5dcc5e25f7a0f9da/tests/test_demo.py)

### Ink and end-to-end claims

- **Protocol adaptation and target-scroll adaptation measure different things.** The author of villa PR 2512 reports that transfer fitted on a registered PHerc. 0814 pair increased mean ROC-AUC by 0.024 without mirror TTA and 0.011 with it, across five native PHerc. 0139 evaluation segments and three matched seeds. The additional self-training arm uses other PHerc. 0139 segments, so that arm measures within-scroll adaptation with held-out segments. A comparison at the same nominal voxel size should record native versus pooled acquisition, energy, surface ID, physical depth range and normalization. [PR and benchmark design](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/pull/2512) · [Native-scan discussion](https://discord.com/channels/1079907749569237093/1571472965273649193)
- **A benchmark win applies to its test set.** Argus v3 reports AUC 0.924 versus 0.913 for Reader v3 on native PHerc. 0841 w00 under matched inference, and the pooled validation-mask comparison reports 0.902 versus 0.907. The repository describes four weight-averaged fine-tunes. The thread includes a separate visual check on a small native area, where stronger responses included both faint strokes and blank background, and the author discusses reserving an untouched final test set. Near-chance performance on reversed layers is evidence of depth dependence on that case. Check nearby wrong-sheet and blank controls to test whether the strokes themselves are specific. [Argus results and recipe](https://github.com/ruiconte/ink-swarm) · [Discussion and follow-up](https://discord.com/channels/1079907749569237093/1572356121904291900)
- **Reader runtime, tiling and weights all affect comparability.** RV2-Swift reports 62.8 s versus 32.0 s on PHerc. 0139 w043 on an NVIDIA L4 with GPU normalization and a larger 384-pixel Reader v2 tile. The larger tile changes the output. The histogram PR reports byte-identical output at fixed tiling on its tested PHerc. 0139 w054 Reader v3 run on an RTX 3080. Those are two different experiments. [RV2-Swift measurements](https://github.com/armando-gaona/rv2-swift) · [Histogram implementation and measurements](https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/pull/2521)
- **Scope the claim to the delivered geometry and reading.** PHerc. 1667 is the published semi-automatic reconstruction and expert reading of a preserved portion. UFSM and HerculeX can run candidate geometry pipelines against whole volumes, and ScrollFiesta starts with a surface-prediction volume. Each should be judged by physical winding continuity, supported area and evidence of readable text on the same region. For example, the four-scroll track release gives one 8,000-step run per scroll and three middle windings per fit with renders, while its authors report no readable letters in the reviewed Reader v3 images. That is evidence for a fit-and-render workflow with a negative ink result. [PHerc. 1667 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29085v1#Sx4) · [Four-scroll release and scope](https://github.com/Ahggggg/spiral-tracks-four-scrolls)
